Evaluate each consequence by its net effect on aggregate welfare, summed across
all affected stakeholders with equal weight per unit of welfare. Distribution is
not itself a consideration; a gain to the well-off and an equal loss to the
worse-off net to zero.

## Sign scale (-2..+2)

- **+2** large net welfare gain in aggregate
- **+1** modest net welfare gain
- **0** welfare-neutral in aggregate
- **-1** modest net welfare loss
- **-2** large net welfare loss in aggregate
