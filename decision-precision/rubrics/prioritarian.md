Evaluate each consequence by its effect on welfare, giving extra moral weight to
benefits and harms that fall on the worse-off. The same-sized welfare change
counts for more the lower the baseline position of the affected stakeholder, so
a harm concentrated on already-disadvantaged incumbents is weighted more heavily
than an equal harm to the advantaged.

## Sign scale (-2..+2)

- **+2** large priority-weighted welfare gain (esp. to the worse-off)
- **+1** modest priority-weighted gain
- **0** priority-weighted neutral
- **-1** modest priority-weighted loss
- **-2** large priority-weighted loss (esp. concentrated on the worse-off)
